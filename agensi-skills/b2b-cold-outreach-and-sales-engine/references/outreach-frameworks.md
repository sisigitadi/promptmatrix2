# B2B Outreach and Deliverability Frameworks Reference

This reference documents copywriting structures, deliverability factors, and trigger avoidance guidelines for cold B2B communications.

## 1. High-Deliverability Subject Line Archetypes

| Archetype | Format & Example | Typical Open Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Observation / Trigger** | `{{first_name}} / {{company_trigger}}` (e.g. "alex / new hiring batch") | 68% - 78% |
| **Internal Question** | `quick question on {{topic}}` (e.g. "quick question on ci pipeline") | 62% - 74% |
| **Common Ground** | `fellow {{group}} member` (e.g. "fellow saas founder") | 58% - 70% |

Rules:
- Strictly lowercase or title-case.
- No punctuation at the end of the subject line.
- Avoid numbers or percentages in subject lines unless discussing verified internal benchmarks.

## 2. Inbound Spam Trigger Words to Eliminate

The following words trigger spam filters and must be replaced:

| Banned Outreach Words | Recommended Authentic Replacements |
| :--- | :--- |
| `guarantee` / `guaranteed` | `demonstrated`, `verified in production` |
| `free trial` / `free consultation` | `open to reviewing`, `take a look` |
| `urgent` / `act now` | `if this is currently on your radar` |
| Overhyped tech superlatives | `specific approach`, `tested method` |
| `100% risk-free` | `low-commitment review` |
