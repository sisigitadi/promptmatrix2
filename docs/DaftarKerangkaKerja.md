# 📋 Katalog & Daftar Kerangka Kerja PromptMatrix 2.0 (136+ Formula)

PromptMatrix 2.0 menyediakan lebih dari 136 formula prompt terstruktur yang terbagi ke dalam 8 kategori utama:

---

## 1. ✍️ Teks & Konten (Text & Content)
- **Konten & Pemasaran**: Hook-Story-Offer (HSO) Video Pendek, Generator 10 Hook Viral Pembuka Video, Penulis Thread & Utas Edukasi (Twitter & Threads), Skrip Voiceover Storytelling Video (POV / Mini-Vlog), Penulis Thread Media Sosial, Skrip Video Pendek (TikTok/Reels).
- **Pemasaran & Penjualan**: Deskripsi Produk Marketplace (Shopee & Tokopedia), Customer Service & Closing Sales Chat WhatsApp, Copywriting Iklan (Ad Copy), Email Marketing & Penjualan, Generator Skrip Video Sales Letter (VSL).
- **Komunikasi Profesional**: Email Penawaran Kerjasama & Endorsement, Pembuat Proposal Jasa Freelance (1 Halaman), Penulis Email Kantor Diplomatik (Situasi Sensitif), Optimalisasi CV & Resume Lolos ATS, Penulis Siaran Pers (Press Release).
- **Akademis & Edukasi**: Feynman Technique Explainer (Analogi Sederhana), Ringkasan Dokumen Panjang & PDF (TL;DR + Action Items), Pembuat Kuis & Soal Ujian Interaktif, Generator Rencana Pembelajaran (Lesson Plan), Penyederhana Teks Akademik.
- **Kehidupan & Produktivitas**: Perencana Menu & Resep dari Bahan Kulkas, Simulasi Anggaran Keuangan Bulanan (50-30-20), Manajemen Waktu & Prioritas Harian.
- **Branding & Identitas**: Generator Nama Usaha & Slogan Catchy, Penulis Kisah Merek (Brand Storytelling).

---

## 2. 🎨 Gambar & Desain (Image & Design)
- **Framework Prompt Gambar Master**: 3D Disney Pixar Character & Avatar Style, Foto Produk Mewah Fotorealistik (Studio Photography), Miniatur Ruangan 3D Isometrik (Isometric Diorama Room), Estetika Foto Analog 90s Vintage Film (Polaroid / 35mm), Desain Logo Vektor Minimalis & Modern, Midjourney Master Prompt Formula, DALL-E 3 Precision Formula, Flux.1 Cinematic Realism Formula.

---

## 3. 🎬 Video & Animasi (Video & Animation)
- **Generator Video AI**: Runway Gen-3 / Sora / Kling Cinematic Director Prompt, Text-to-Video Camera Motion Prompts, Video Transition & Lighting Prompts.

---

## 4. 🎵 Audio & Musik (Audio & Music)
- **Prompt AI Musik & Audio**: Suno / Udio Song Structure Prompts, Voiceover Style & Acoustic Sound Prompts.

---

## 5. 💻 Kode & Pengembang (Code & Development)
- **Utilitas & Development**: Frontend Component Architect (Tailwind/React), Backend API Endpoint Designer, Code Refactoring & Bug Fixer, SQL Query & Database Schema Architect.

---

## 6. ⚡ Prompt Ringkas (Concise Prompts)
- **Kerangka Kerja Terstruktur Populer**: RTF (Role-Task-Format), TAG (Task-Action-Goal), BAB (Before-After-Bridge), PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve), AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action), STAR (Situation-Task-Action-Result), CARE, APE, TRACE, CO-STAR.
- **Pemikiran Terstruktur & Analisis**: Chain-of-Thought (CoT), Tree-of-Thoughts (ToT), Analisis SWOT Personal & Strategi Karir, First Principles Thinking.

---

## 7. 📁 Prompt Proyek (Project Prompts)
- **Desain & Pengembangan Proyek**: End-to-End Project Brief, Product Requirement Document (PRD), Rencana Peluncuran Produk (Product Launch Roadmap).

---

## 8. 💡 Koleksi & Inovasi (Collection & Innovation)
- **Metodologi Inovasi & Brainstorming**: SCAMPER Method (Inovasi Produk & Ide Kreatif), Blue Ocean Strategy (Skema 4 Tindakan ERRC), Design Thinking (5 Tahap Solusi Human-Centered), Brainstorming Ide Bisnis Modal Kecil (Side Hustle).
- **Arsitektur Multi-Agent & Sistem Mandiri**: Multi-Agent Orchestration (Manager, Researcher, Coder, Critic), ReAct Framework (Reasoning + Acting Workflow), Reverse Prompt Engineering.
